Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

1. Overview

VeriFairy is a Discord application that helps participating Discord servers manage access to age-restricted areas through user-selected third-party verification providers.

This Privacy Policy explains the limited information VeriFairy processes, why it is processed, how it is used, and how to contact us about privacy questions or requests.

2. Information VeriFairy Processes

To start, complete, and manage a verification request, VeriFairy may process limited operational information:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • The Discord server or guild ID where verification was requested
  • The Discord role connected to verification access
  • A provider-generated verification session or reference ID
  • Verification status, such as pending, verified, denied, expired, or cancelled
  • Dates and times related to the verification request and result
  • Limited technical, security, and error logs needed to operate and protect the service

3. Information VeriFairy Does Not Collect

VeriFairy does not ask you to send identity documents or sensitive verification materials through Discord.

VeriFairy does not intentionally collect, receive, or store:

  • Government-issued identity documents
  • Driver's license, passport, or state ID numbers
  • Document images or scans
  • Selfies, biometric data, or liveness-check materials
  • Date of birth
  • Social Security numbers
  • Payment-card or banking information

If a server uses an external verification provider, any documents or verification materials are submitted directly on that provider's website, not to VeriFairy through Discord.

4. How Information Is Used

VeriFairy uses the limited information above to:

  • Create and track a verification request initiated through the bot
  • Match a provider result to the correct Discord account and server
  • Grant, deny, revoke, or manage the applicable Discord role
  • Prevent abuse, duplicate requests, and unauthorized role assignment
  • Troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and respond to support requests

VeriFairy does not sell user information and does not use verification information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or data brokerage.

5. Third-Party Services

VeriFairy may rely on third-party services, including:

  • Discord: Provides the platform, Discord account functionality, server functionality, and role-management features.
  • User-selected verification providers: A user may choose an available third-party provider to complete age verification on that provider's own website or service.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: VeriFairy may use third-party providers for website hosting, application hosting, storage, security, and related infrastructure when necessary to operate the service.

When you open a verification link, the provider selected for that session controls its own verification process. Its terms and privacy policy govern the information you submit to that provider.

6. When Information Is Shared

VeriFairy shares limited information only when needed to operate the service:

  • With Discord, to operate the bot and assign the appropriate role
  • With the verification provider selected for your verification session, only as needed to create and receive that session's outcome
  • With hosting or security providers necessary to operate the service
  • When required by applicable law, legal process, or a legitimate security need

VeriFairy does not sell, rent, or share user information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Data Retention

VeriFairy retains limited verification and operational records only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide verification access, operate and secure the application, prevent abuse, resolve support requests, and meet legal obligations.

VeriFairy is currently under development. Specific automated retention and deletion periods may be added as the application develops. If VeriFairy makes a material change to its retention practices, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that change takes effect.

8. Security

VeriFairy uses reasonable safeguards designed to protect the limited operational information it processes. These may include restricted access, environment-variable secret management, provider-webhook signature verification, and data-minimization practices.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send identity documents, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information to VeriFairy through Discord.

9. Your Requests and Choices

You may contact us to ask about the limited operational information associated with your Discord account or request deletion where applicable. Requests may be limited where retention is necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or server administration.

To make a privacy or deletion request, email verifairysupport@gmail.com.

A server administrator may remove your verified role at any time. Removing a role does not automatically delete all operational records. Contact us if you want to request deletion.

10. Eligibility

VeriFairy is not intended for people who do not meet Discord's minimum age requirement or the minimum age required for the application in their location. Do not use VeriFairy if you are not eligible to use Discord, the server, or the selected verification provider.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VeriFairy or its data practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
